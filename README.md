# Glacier influence on antibiotic resistance gene distribution in high-altitude lakes


## Scripts

- `01_ARG_analysis.R`  
  Analysis of antibiotic resistance gene composition and abundance.

- `02_Statistical_analysis.R`  
  Statistical analyses.

- `03_ARG-MGE_gene_arrangement_plots.R`  
  Visualization of ARG–MGE gene arrangements.

- `04_ARG_MGE_Procrustes_analysis.R`  
  Procrustes analysis of ARGs and mobile genetic elements.

- `05_Piecewise_SEM.R`  
  Piecewise structural equation modeling.

- `06_MAG_relative_abundance.R`  
  Analysis and visualization of MAG relative abundance.

- `07_Similarity_distance_decay.R`  
  Bray–Curtis similarity and geographic distance-decay analysis.

- `08_Glacier_source_contribution.R`  
  Glacier-associated source contribution analysis.

- `09_Risk_map.R`  
  Spatial visualization of ARG-related risk.
